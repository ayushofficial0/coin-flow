YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL

Settings & data

Manage your local records and keep a backup.

Data storage

Records are stored in your browser's local storage.

This prototype does not connect to banks or sync across devices. If you clear browser data or switch devices, records may be lost. Export a CSV backup regularly.

Privacy: do not use this prototype to store bank passwords, card numbers or other highly sensitive data. It is a local tracker, not a secure financial institution or encrypted cloud account.
